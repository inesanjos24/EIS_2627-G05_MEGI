The Process

How Relay works.

  1. Place your order

    Register your products and set origin and destination with GPS coordinates on the map.

  2. A courier accepts

    A Staff-validated courier takes the order with a vehicle sized for its weight and volume.

  3. Follow it live

    Watch the status move from Initiated to In Progress on your client page.

  4. Proof of delivery

    Every delivery is confirmed with a digital signature or a photo (e-POD).
